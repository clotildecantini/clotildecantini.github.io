I am a pre-PhD research assistant at [CERMICS](https://cermics.enpc.fr/), École des Ponts ParisTech, where I work on airline revenue management and, more specifically, on competition-aware dynamic pricing algorithms.

My background sits at the intersection of operations research and machine learning. I hold a master's degree in operations research (MPRO, ENSTA Paris) and an engineering degree in mathematics and computer science from École des Ponts ParisTech. Along the way I have worked on railway traffic optimization at SNCF, self-supervised learning for satellite image time series at LIPADE, privacy-preserving speech processing at Callyope, and applied machine learning at Swiss Life.

## Research interests

- **Operations research and optimization**: dynamic pricing, revenue management, combinatorial optimization, metaheuristics
- **Machine learning for decision making**: learning-based approaches to combinatorial and scheduling problems
- **Representation learning**: self-supervised methods for spatio-temporal data
- **Privacy-preserving signal processing**: homomorphic encryption and quantization for audio
